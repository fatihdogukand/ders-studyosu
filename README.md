# Sohbet Okulu

> Turning long study chats and messy notes into clean, calm, printable study cards.

**Sohbet Okulu** is a lightweight, personal study workspace created by a university student looking for a faster, more practical way to actually *study* from AI conversations, lecture notes, and problem solutions—without losing formulas, tables, or sanity along the way.

## Why It Exists

Studying with AI tools is great until you have to scroll through endless chats just to find one derivation, broken equation, or comparison table before an exam. I built Sohbet Okulu for my own daily study routine so I could:

- Keep formulas, tables, code, and multiple-choice questions readable instead of mangled.
- Organize notes into folders, highlight key points, pin tricky cards for review, and track study progress.
- Switch smoothly between desktop, tablet, and phone, or export clean A4 pages when it's time to print.
